# BikeDekho Data Analytics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer demographics and purchasing behavior to understand the factors influencing bike purchases.

The dataset contains information about customers, including age, gender, income, marital status, education, occupation, home ownership, car ownership, commute distance, and bike purchase decisions.

Using Microsoft Excel, the project explores customer segments and purchasing patterns through data cleaning, pivot tables, and dashboard analysis.

The objective is to identify potential customer groups and generate business recommendations for targeted marketing and customer engagement.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, analysis, pivot tables, and dashboard development.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Customer Purchase Analysis
- Distribution of bike buyers and non-buyers.
- Overall bike purchase behavior.

### 2. Income Analysis
- Bike purchasing patterns across income groups.
- Relationship between income and purchasing decisions.

### 3. Demographic Analysis
- Customer age and gender distribution.
- Marital status and education patterns.
- Occupation-based purchasing behavior.

### 4. Lifestyle Analysis
- Home ownership and car ownership.
- Commute distance and bike purchasing decisions.
- Regional purchasing patterns.

## Key Insights
- 481 individuals (48.1%) purchased a bike.
- 519 individuals (51.9%) did not purchase a bike.
- Bike purchases are concentrated in income brackets between ₹20,000 and ₹60,000.
- Clerical and Professional occupations show higher participation.
- Customers with shorter commute distances (0–5 miles) show higher bike purchasing patterns.
- Demographic and lifestyle factors provide useful segmentation opportunities.

## Business Recommendations
- Develop targeted campaigns for relevant age and customer segments.
- Personalize product recommendations based on income and commute distance.
- Explore financing options for customers in the ₹20,000–₹60,000 income bracket.
- Introduce location-based promotions and dealership partnerships.
- Promote accessories and additional services to relevant customer groups.

## Project Files
- `bikedekho_data_analytics.xlsx` – Raw data, cleaned data, pivot tables, and dashboard.
- `Bikedekho Data Analysis Insights Report.pdf` – Detailed findings and recommendations.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how customer data can be used to understand purchasing behavior and identify meaningful customer segments. The analysis provides insights that can support targeted marketing, customer personalization, and improved business decision-making.
